Eight months before the gates open, Saturday is sold out. Thank you —
that has never happened this early.

If you still want to be there on Saturday, a **weekend ticket** gets you
in on all three days, and there are a few hundred left. Friday evening
and Sunday tickets are on sale as normal.

Please don't buy tickets from strangers online. If you can't come after
all, you can sell your ticket through the resale page of our ticket shop,
at the price you paid, and the buyer gets a new ticket in their own name.
