---
title: A greenhouse becomes a dance tent
date: 2026-08-20
description: The old tomato greenhouse behind the dyke gets a second life as the Kas stage, covered, late and with room for 1,500.
image: greenhouse-tent
---

For a hundred years, the long glasshouse behind the dyke grew tomatoes.
It has stood empty since 2019. From next summer it will be the **Kas**,
our new covered stage for electronic music, open until two in the
morning on Friday and Saturday.

We are keeping the glass, the iron frame and the old heating pipes. The
farmer who owns it, Jan de Wit, grew up a field away from the festival
site and came to his first Brakwater in 2013.

> "My grandfather built it. I'd rather it was full of people dancing than standing empty."

The Kas also gives us a dry place when it rains, which, on the Dutch
coast in July, it sometimes does.
