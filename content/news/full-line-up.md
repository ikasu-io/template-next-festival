Here it is: 24 acts from nine countries, on the dyke, in the greenhouse
and on the beach.

**Northern Lull** open the weekend on Friday night, their first
open-air show in the Netherlands. **Tidewater Radio** close Saturday on
the Dijk with songs from their new record. And on Sunday, **Aurélie &
the Eastern Wind** bring eleven musicians and a choir for the last set of
the weekend, timed for the sunset.

In the Kas, Friday and Saturday run until two in the morning, with
Vela Nine and Selma Ruiz playing the long closing sets. On the Strand
stage it's folk, jazz and chanson every afternoon, and the Puddle Jumpers
for the youngest visitors on Sunday.

And, as every year, the Fanfare Zoutkeet open Sunday on the dyke.

The timetable is up too. Tap the star next to an act to build your own
plan; it stays on your phone.
