The last of the stages came down on Wednesday, and the beach looks like
nothing happened. Some numbers from Brakwater 2026:

- 12,000 visitors over three days
- 4,100 people who came by train and shuttle bus, and 3,800 by bike
- 61,000 refills at the free water taps
- 1 thunderstorm, on Saturday at 18:40, and a 25-minute break
- 340 volunteers, who made all of it happen
- 0 lost children who stayed lost for more than ten minutes

The dates for next year: **9, 10 and 11 July 2027**. See you on the dyke.
